### Repeated/concurrent runs and drift (first half of November)

- `Aos.ReplayCli repeat-diagnostics` reruns Planner v2 over the pinned dataset `--runs` times at `--concurrency`. Each run uses its own
  pool, run id and staging directory, writes signed artifacts, and is replayed from them.
- Per-case outcome signature (status, reason, attempts, diagnosis, evidence kind/path/line/content hash, scores, stages, tool-call rows,
  confidence; excluding run ids, timestamps and latency) must be identical across runs: `run-stability`.
- `DiagnosticsDrift` (Core): one-sided CUSUM, nearest-rank percentiles. The `stage-failures` series (target 0) alarms on a single failed
  iteration and gates the run. `tool-latency` and `planner-overhead` series are relative to a baseline window and are reported but do not gate,
  because wall-clock latency is host noise.
- Planner overhead, tool duration and sandbox overhead are reported as separate p50/p95/max summaries.

### Limits

- CUSUM thresholds are hand-set defaults, not tuned; the stage-failure series is only ever exercised by synthetic injected failures.
  Real regressions have not occurred in the retained runs, so the false-alarm rate is untested.
- The dataset is deterministic, so repeated runs mostly confirm reproducibility; they cannot show planner variability until a model candidate exists.
- Concurrency is bounded to one host; this is not a throughput benchmark.
